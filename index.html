What is real: This file contains the learned weights of a small, word-level causal transformer (24 features, 2 attention heads, 4 layers, RoPE, residual additions, GELU MLP). It runs entirely offline in JavaScript. Its 70-word vocabulary was trained on short original story fragments, so the sentences are simple and may repeat. It is not a general-purpose language model. The prefill and incremental-generation paths use the same mathematical operations; during generation the app reuses the K/V cache instead of recomputing old columns. The maximum model window is 24 tokens; beyond that, the oldest tokens are dropped and the cache rebuilt.
